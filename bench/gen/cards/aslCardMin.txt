ASL:

fn pick a: Int64 xs: (List Int64) -> (Option Int64)
  (match (listHead xs)
    ((none) (none))
    ((some h) (if (= (mod h 2) 0) (some (+ h a)) (pick a (listDrop xs 1)))))

Mistakes:
1. body is ONE parenthesised form under a bare fn line
2. = and mod, never == or %
3. balance parentheses
4. (listAppend xs (list x)); (list) is empty
5. camelCase names: listLength, stringFromInt64, (str a b); mapGet gives (some v) or (none)
